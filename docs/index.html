Auto Job

A free, open-source dashboard that runs your job search on your own computer.

Get it on GitHub Privacy policy

What it does

  • Finds new jobs every day and scores each one against your resume.
  • Rewrites your resume for the jobs worth applying to, using only facts from your own resume.
  • Emails the application from your Gmail, with your resume and a cover letter attached.
  • Watches your inbox for employer replies, drafts an answer for you to review (it never sends replies itself), and adds a calendar reminder.
  • Prepares you for interviews and writes emails and cover letters.

Runs on your computer

Auto Job is software you download and run yourself. There is no Auto Job server and no account to create. Your resume, applications and job history stay on your own computer.

Why it asks for Google access

Auto Job connects to your own Gmail and Google Calendar through n8n, which runs on your computer. It uses that access only to send the applications you approve, read job-alert and employer-reply emails, create reply drafts, and add calendar reminders. See the privacy policy for details.

Auto Job is open source under the MIT license. Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
