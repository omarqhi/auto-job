Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Auto Job is free, open-source software that you download and run on your own computer. This policy explains what it does with your information, including the data it reads from your Google account.

Who runs it

You do. Auto Job has no central server and no user accounts. The developer does not receive, collect or store any of your data, and cannot see what you do with the software.

Google account data it uses

When you connect your Google account (through n8n, which also runs on your computer), Auto Job uses it only for these purposes:

  • Gmail, send: to send the job applications and emails you choose to send, with your resume and cover letter attached.
  • Gmail, read: to read job-alert emails and replies from employers to your applications, so it can find new jobs and tell you when an employer writes back.
  • Gmail, drafts: to save a suggested reply in your Drafts. It never sends replies on its own.
  • Google Calendar: to add a reminder event when an employer replies.

Auto Job's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Where your data is stored

Your resume, job listings, applications, email text and settings are stored only on your own computer. Your Google sign-in is stored by n8n on your computer.

Who your data is shared with

  • Employers: the applications you send, by email.
  • Anthropic (Claude): your resume, job postings and the emails it helps you write are sent to Claude, under your own Claude account, to score jobs and write text. They are not used to train models by Auto Job.
  • Apify: job-search terms are sent to Apify, under your own Apify account, to find job listings.

Auto Job does not sell your data, does not use it for advertising, and does not share it with anyone else.

Deleting your data

Delete the Auto Job folder from your computer. To remove Google access, open your Google account permissions and remove the app.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on GitHub.
